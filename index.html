00 · Read first

This is a funding system, not a contact list

MIT affiliation is a relationship filter. It is not proof of investment authority, cloud-provider access or credit eligibility. The report therefore runs two parallel lanes and only joins them where evidence supports the join.

Capital lane

Identify MIT-connected investors with current AI activity and early-stage relevance. A signed investment may strengthen the Google manual review, but it does not automatically issue AWS, GCP or model credits.

Provider lane

Verify an actual provider or partner mechanism: AWS Activate Org ID, Google partner referral, Microsoft Founders Hub, NVIDIA Inception, or a named accelerator credit stack. The provider must be evidenced separately.

Execution lane

Use the existing C3A credit work: AWS runway, expired GCP appeal, NVIDIA Capital Connect, Microsoft, Cloudflare, IndiaAI/DPIIT and the VC proof needed to reopen higher tiers.

Binding truth

Older internal files contain useful leads but some amounts, partner labels and status claims are not re-verified in this MIT wave. They remain source inputs, not automatic facts. Current provider pages and the corrections ledger override legacy shorthand.
